110 – 120 oz / day

Fluid retention is highest when daily water intake fluctuates. A constant 110–120 oz daily intake flushes aldosterone, stabilizes baseline readings, and prevents artificial 5+ lb scale spikes.

Ultramarathon Performance Tips & Tricks Vault

Battle-tested strategies for pacing, NordicTrack X11i power-hiking, race-day fueling, and 24-hour looped execution.

1. Pacing & Heart Rate Rules

Run/Walk Automation (4:1 Split)

In San Angelo, do not run continuously just because it is flat. Implement a 4 min run / 1 min walk split from Loop 1. Preserves muscle glycogen and keeps core temperature down.

Heart Rate Cap (Zone 2)

Never exceed Zone 2 (below 75% Max HR) during the first 60% of any ultra. If you cannot speak a full sentence cleanly, slow down immediately.

2. Incline & Downhill Quad Conditioning

Power-Hiking Mechanics (X11i 15%–40%)

On gradients above 10–12%, running burns 50% more glycogen for minimal speed gains. Drive hands into thighs, keep chest open, push through heels, long powerful strides.

Downhill Eccentric Quad Training

Colorado ultras are lost on downhills. Eccentric contractions shred quads. Use X11i decline feature (-3%) or local trail descents to condition quads to absorb impact.
