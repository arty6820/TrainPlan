Daily Nutritional Protocol

The "Fixed Family Dinner" Execution Strategy

Because family dinners and weekend meals are fixed variables, fat loss is driven by controlling the Monday–Friday daytime window and applying quiet portion mechanics at dinner:

1 The Half-Plate Rule

Before touching entrees or starches at dinner, fill half your plate with vegetables/salad and eat them first to blunt insulin spikes and increase satiety.

2 The Dinner Starch Swap

Take half your normal portion of pasta, rice, or potatoes on run days; skip dinner starches entirely on non-run/incline-walk days (Tuesdays/Thursdays) and take extra protein instead.

3 The Kitchen-Closed Boundary

Zero tasting while cooking, zero finishing kids' leftovers, and zero post-dinner snacking. Water or herbal tea only after dinner.

4 Fasted Morning Base Runs

Perform easy Zone 2 runs under 75 minutes fasted (black coffee + water/electrolytes only) to maximize fat oxidation. Reserve pre-run carbs strictly for Wednesday tempo/speed workouts and Saturday mountain long runs.

5 Workday Protein Anchor & Hydration Lock

Lock in 135–145g of protein daily via repeatable, lean breakfasts and lunches (egg whites/eggs, chicken, turkey, tuna, greens) while keeping daily hydration strictly at 110–120 oz.

Section 3 • Mountain Race Execution

Ultramarathon & Multi-Race Performance Playbook

Core tactical doctrine governing your Spring Colorado Ultra (Apr 24) and consecutive Summer Racing Circuit (June 5, July 10, August 7).

Rule 01 • Pacing Discipline

The "Too Slow" Start

In 100Ks and 100-milers, start with an immediate run/walk discipline: power-hike every grade over 10–12%, and run smooth flats/rollers strictly in Zone 2. Burning matches in the first 15 miles guarantees a mid-pack fade.

Rule 02 • Eccentric Resilience

Downhill Quad Conditioning

Colorado ultras are won on descents. Use the decline setting (-3%) on your treadmill or fast local trail descents during base blocks to build eccentric quad resilience and prevent late-race quad blowout.

Rule 03 • Metabolic & Electrolyte Fueling

Caloric & Sodium Precision

Consume 250–300 kcal/hr starting at Minute 45—transitioning from gels/drink mix early to real foods (potatoes, broth, rice balls, quesadillas) after Hour 5—alongside 500–700mg sodium/hr.

Rule 04 • Summer Circuit Survival

Back-to-Back Race Rule (Jun / Jul / Aug)

After finishing a 100K/100M in June, July, or August, take 5–7 full days of zero running. You cannot build fitness in the 3 weeks between summer races—you can only lose fitness by returning to running too early while tendons are still inflamed.
